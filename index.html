Beginner earning guides

A practical route from learning a tool to offering a useful service.

GUIDE 01 · DESIGN

Offer social media designs

Learn to create consistent posts and simple promotional creatives for local businesses.

Starter action plan

Make 5 sample designs for imaginary businesses, assemble a portfolio, then contact relevant businesses politely. Agree on scope and price before starting paid work.

GUIDE 02 · VIDEO

Edit short-form videos

Practice clean cuts, readable captions, good pacing, and correct aspect ratios.

Starter action plan

Create 3 demo edits using footage you own or have permission to use. Show before/after examples and offer a clearly defined trial project.

GUIDE 03 · CONTENT

Write content packages

Use AI for first drafts, then fact-check and tailor captions, hooks, and content calendars.

Starter action plan

Build a sample 7-day content calendar for one niche. Personalize every draft, verify claims, and never promise viral reach or guaranteed sales.

Your first goal: get one real client.

Learn one skill, make a small portfolio, and test whether people want the service before spending money on tools or ads.
